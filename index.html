About us

Veteran-owned Fort Worth local Residential and commercial

Stacks Property Services is a veteran-owned company based right here in Fort Worth, owned by Jeremy Spann and Tom Morrow. The military taught us to finish the job, do it to standard, and never leave a mess for the next person. We run every job that way.

We started by maintaining our own rental homes near TCU. Neighbors and other property owners began asking us to look after their places too, and today we take care of homes and commercial buildings across Fort Worth.

This is our city, and the people we work for are our neighbors. We treat every home and building like it’s on our own street: honest prices, straight answers, and work we’re proud to put our name on.
